Use {This} for the value typed in this cell. {This[-1]} is this column, one row above (like Excel A1 when you are on A2). {This[-1]}+1 increments it. Ranges: SUM({Qty[-2:0]}) is Qty on this row and the two above. Dates are day/month/year: 01/01/2026+1 → 02/01/2026.
